Curtain styles

Choose your heading style

The heading is the top of the curtain, where it meets the rod or track — it shapes how the fabric folds and hangs.

01

Eyelet curtains

Feature metal rings at the top that create large, even folds. A contemporary look that’s easy to open and close, available in a variety of fabrics and colours.

  • Contemporary
  • Easy to open & close

02

Inverted pleats

Tailored and structured, with pleats folding towards the window. Timeless elegance to suit traditional and modern designs, made from lightweight to heavy fabrics.

  • Tailored
  • Traditional or modern

03

Single pleats

Fabric gathered into small pleats at the top. Versatile and suitable for a wide range of interiors, with a clean, polished look. Can be made from lightweight to heavy fabrics.

  • Versatile
  • Clean & polished

04

Dutch pleats

Structured and decorative, with stiffened fabric at the top. A uniform, sophisticated design often used in formal settings, and customisable in a range of fabrics.

  • Structured
  • Formal settings

05

French pleats

Hand-stitched, pinched pleats that create a classic, elegant look, typically with three folds per pleat. Suited to traditional and formal interiors, in lightweight to heavy fabrics.

  • Classic
  • Hand-stitched

06

Pencil pleats

A gathered heading with small, evenly spaced pleats that resemble pencils. Versatile and classic for all kinds of interiors, with adjustable fullness and many fabrics to choose from.

  • Adjustable fullness
  • Many fabrics

07

Motorised curtains

Operated by an electric motor for convenience, especially on hard-to-reach windows. Control them remotely or through smart home systems. Available in a variety of styles and fabrics.

  • Remote control
  • Smart home systems
