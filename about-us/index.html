About Sweta Furnishing

Who we are

Based in Papatoetoe, Auckland, we are a premier provider of high-quality curtains and blinds.

Our company is dedicated to enhancing your living spaces with stylish and functional window treatments. We offer a diverse range of products, including customisable curtains and innovative blinds, tailored to meet the unique needs of our customers.
