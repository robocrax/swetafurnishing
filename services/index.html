Services we offer

Custom curtain design & installation

Custom curtain design

We create bespoke curtains made to your specific window dimensions and designed to match the décor of your room.

  • Fabric — colour, pattern, texture and durability
  • Heading style — pleated, eyelet or rod pocket
  • Linings — such as blackout or thermal
  • Finishing touches — trims, tassels and tiebacks

Curtain installation

We install your curtains for you — mounting the rods or tracks and hanging the curtains to the agreed measurements and design specifications.

  • Precise measuring at your home
  • Quality checked before delivery or installation
  • Rods or tracks mounted and curtains hung
  • Final inspection and any adjustments

Our process

From first chat to final fitting

How your made-to-measure curtains come together, step by step.

  1. 01

    Initial consultation

    It starts with a consultation, where we talk through your needs, preferences and budget for your curtains.

  2. 02

    Measurement and assessment

    We visit your home to take precise measurements of the windows where the curtains will be installed, and assess the style of your space — including the existing décor and architectural features — so your curtains complement the overall design.

  3. 03

    Fabric selection

    Once the measurements are taken, we help you select the fabric for your custom curtains, considering colour, pattern, texture and durability, along with the function and look you want to achieve.

  4. 04

    Design details

    Next, we discuss any additional design details or customisations, such as the heading style (e.g. pleated, eyelet or rod pocket), lining options (e.g. blackout or thermal) and decorative elements (e.g. trims, tassels and tiebacks).

  5. 05

    Quotation and approval

    Based on your specifications, we provide a quotation for your custom-made curtains, including fabric costs, labour and any additional materials or services. Once you approve the quote, production can begin.

  6. 06

    Production

    Your curtains are custom-made to the measurements and design details we discussed. This may involve cutting the fabric to size, sewing and assembling the curtain panels, attaching linings and other elements, and adding any decorative finishes.

  7. 07

    Quality control

    Before delivery or installation, your curtains undergo quality control checks to make sure they meet the specified measurements, design requirements and quality standards.

  8. 08

    Delivery or installation

    Once your curtains are ready, they can be delivered to your home or installed by professionals, depending on what we’ve arranged with you. Installation may involve mounting curtain rods or tracks and hanging the curtains to the measurements and design specifications.

  9. 09

    Final inspection and adjustments

    After installation, you’ll have the opportunity to inspect your curtains to make sure they meet your expectations. Any necessary adjustments can be made at this stage for a perfect fit and finish.
