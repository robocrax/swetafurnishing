Blind types

Find the right blind for every room

From simple roller blinds to insulating honeycomb and fully motorised systems.

01

Vertical blinds

Made of vertical slats (fabric, vinyl or wood) hanging from a top track. Adjustable for light and privacy, and ideal for large windows and sliding doors.

  • Large windows
  • Sliding doors

02

Roller blinds

A single piece of fabric attached to a roller that rolls up and down for light control and privacy. Available in various opacities, colours and patterns — simple, versatile and easy to use.

  • Simple & versatile
  • Range of opacities

03

Motorised blinds

Controlled remotely, or set to adjust automatically based on conditions like time, temperature or sunlight. They use motors, sensors and wireless technology for convenience and energy efficiency.

  • Remote or automatic
  • Energy efficient

04

Honeycomb blinds

Also known as cellular shades, honeycomb blinds have hexagonal cells that trap air for insulation and better energy efficiency. Available in various materials, colours and cell sizes for light filtering and privacy.

  • Insulating
  • Light filtering

05

Roman headrail system

A streamlined mechanism for Roman blinds, with a durable headrail for smooth operation and even weight distribution. Choose cordless or motorised operation — modern convenience with an elegant design, for homes and commercial spaces.

  • Roman blinds
  • Cordless or motorised

06

Wooden blinds

Horizontal wooden slats operated by a cord — tilt them for light control and privacy. A natural, elegant look, available in different wood types, finishes and slat sizes.

  • Natural look
  • Tilt to adjust

07

Aluminium venetian blinds

Horizontal aluminium slats that are lightweight, durable and moisture-resistant. Tilt for light and privacy control. Suitable for kitchens and bathrooms, in various colours and finishes.

  • Moisture-resistant
  • Kitchens & bathrooms

08

Venetian blinds

Horizontal slats made from aluminium, wood or vinyl. Tilt to control light and privacy. Versatile and widely used in homes and offices for both function and style.

  • Homes & offices
  • Versatile
